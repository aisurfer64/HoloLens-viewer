What you’re looking at

This is the camera, but the patent is the software behind it.

Close one eye, then the other, and your finger seems to jump. That’s parallax. In surgery, the same shift throws off the device’s measurements of the anatomy.

The patented software reads fiducial markers, fixed reference points on the instrument, and corrects the shift so every measurement stays true.
